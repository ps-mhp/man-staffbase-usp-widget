# Step by Step

1. Insert the **USP box** onto the page and open its
   settings. The editor fills the entire screen.
2. On the left are the **bullet points**. Click **New Bullet Point** and enter
   a **keyword** in the middle and, optionally, an **explanation**. Repeat
   this for each benefit (up to eight; three to four read
   best).
3. Optional: Under **Icon**, click **Select Icon…**. The icon library opens
   on the right; the search finds German and English names,
   e.g., “truck” or “calendar,” and the filter separates Craft, MAN, and shapes. A
   click applies the icon; **No Icon** removes it.
4. Rearrange the items by dragging the handle in the list or using the arrows
   above the form. You can also duplicate or delete an item there.
5. On the right side of the **USP Box** panel, enter the **heading** under **Content**,
   such as “What’s in it for the customer?”, and, if needed, a **line above it**,
   such as “Customer benefits.”
6. Under **Design**, select **Layout**, **Accent**, and whether to show **Icons**;
   under **Behavior**, enable **Builder Mode**.
7. Use **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile** at the top to check how the box appears
   on the page.
8. Click **Apply** and save the page. **Cancel**
   discards the changes; **Undo** reverses the last step.
